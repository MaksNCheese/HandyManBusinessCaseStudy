About Almazone Services

Family-Owned & Community Focused

Almazone Services was built on a simple idea: homeowners deserve honest workmanship, dependable service, and results they can trust. With more than 20 years of handyman experience, we have helped customers complete everything from everyday repairs and installations to custom projects designed around their specific vision.

As a family-owned business, we take pride in treating every project as if it were our own home. We focus on accuracy, quality, and long-term results rather than rushing through a job. Whether it's mounting a television, assembling furniture, installing lighting, repairing drywall, maintaining ventilation systems, replacing faucets, hanging mirrors and artwork, or creating completely unique projects, we approach every task with care and attention to detail.

One of the values that defines our business is adaptability. We believe there is always something new to learn, and we are constantly expanding our knowledge to take on new challenges and provide more solutions for our customers. If a project requires a specialized approach, we take the time to understand it and complete it properly.

Today, Almazone Services proudly serves homeowners throughout Miami-Dade and surrounding areas. As we continue to grow, we are also preparing to expand into additional service divisions while maintaining the same commitment to quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction that helped build our reputation.

Built On Reliability

Behind Almazone Services is a master handyman with more than 20 years of hands-on experience in repairs, installations, maintenance, and custom home improvement projects. What began as a passion for building, fixing, and creating has grown into a business dedicated to helping homeowners solve problems and improve their spaces.

Known for his attention to detail and commitment to doing things correctly, he believes quality workmanship takes priority over rushing a project. From simple household repairs to unique custom builds, he approaches every job with patience, professionalism, and a willingness to find the right solution. When faced with something new, he embraces the challenge, learns what is needed, and adapts to deliver the best possible result for his customers.
