Frequently Asked Questions

Services & Projects

What types of work do you do?

We handle repairs, installations, maintenance, TV mounting, drywall repair, painting, carpentry, furniture assembly, custom projects, and many other home improvement tasks.

Do you only perform handyman work?

Our primary focus is handyman services, repairs, installations, maintenance, and custom home projects. We continuously expand our capabilities to provide additional solutions for homeowners.

Can you help with projects that aren't listed on the website?

Yes. Many of our projects are unique. If your project isn't listed, contact us and we'll discuss whether it is a good fit.

Do I need to provide materials?

That depends on the project. Some customers already have materials, while others prefer us to help source what is needed.

Pricing & Estimates

Do you provide free estimates?

Yes. Contact us with information about your project and we'll discuss the scope of work and provide an estimate.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is different. Pricing depends on the scope of work, materials, installation requirements, and project complexity. Contact us for a personalized estimate.

Scheduling

How do I schedule service?

Simply use the contact form or call us directly. We'll discuss the project and schedule a convenient time.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Hialeah, Miami, Miami Lakes, Doral, Kendall, Homestead, Hollywood, and surrounding Miami-Dade communities.
